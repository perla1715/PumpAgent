# PumpAgent

## Project Vision

PumpAgent is not a trading bot.

PumpAgent is an AI Learning-First Trading Agent whose primary mission is to understand how market structure evolves before, during and after explosive price movements.

The goal of the project is not to maximize profits.

The goal is to build an intelligent system that continuously learns from the market and gradually approaches the reasoning process of an experienced professional trader.

---

## Core Philosophy

Price is only the visible result.

The real market exists behind the chart.

PumpAgent studies both:

- Market Structure
- Market Participation

Neither one is sufficient alone.

Only together can they explain what the market is doing.

---

## Learning First

Every interesting situation becomes a learning case.

PumpAgent never wastes market experience.

Every pump...

Every failure...

Every continuation...

Every liquidation...

becomes part of its growing knowledge.

---

## Human + AI

The system is built around collaboration.

Human provides:

- experience
- intuition
- review
- strategic direction

AI provides:

- continuous observation
- memory
- consistency
- objective analysis

The human always has the final decision.

---

## Long-Term Vision

PumpAgent should evolve from a simple market scanner into a complete multi-agent trading intelligence system.

Every future module must respect this vision.
